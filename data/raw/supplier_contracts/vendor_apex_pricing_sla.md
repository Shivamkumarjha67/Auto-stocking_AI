## 1. Vendor Profile

Apex Supply Co. is AutoStock-AI's balanced mid-tier hardware vendor, positioned between TechCorp Logistics' premium/fast profile and GlobalParts Direct's bulk/slow profile. Apex offers moderate pricing, a flat and predictable MOQ, and standard lead times without the extreme volume discounts or extreme wait times seen at either end of the vendor spectrum. Apex is contracted exclusively for the Hardware category (SKU-2001–SKU-2005) and does not currently supply Electronics SKUs.

- **Standard Lead Time:** 7–10 business days from PO confirmation
- **Expedited Fulfillment:** Not offered under this contract tier
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** Flat 50 units across all contracted SKUs
- **Payment Terms:** Net 45 from invoice date
- **Late Delivery Penalty:** None specified in current contract (see Section 6)

## 4. Lead Time Structure

- Standard lead time window across the SKU-2001–SKU-2005 catalog is **7–10 business days**, with per-SKU variation depending on manufacturing and assembly complexity (see Section 2).
- No expedited fulfillment tier is currently contracted with Apex Supply Co. Orders requiring sub-7-day delivery should be evaluated against TechCorp Logistics (VEND-001) if the required SKU overlaps, or escalated per internal procurement policy for alternate sourcing.
- Lead time is measured from written PO confirmation, not from initial quote or RFQ submission.

---

## 5. Payment Terms

- **Standard Terms:** Net 45 from invoice date.
- **Accepted Payment Methods:** ACH and wire transfer.
- **Early Payment Discount:** None currently offered under this contract tier.
- **Deposit Requirement:** None — Apex does not require deposits for standard or bulk tier orders.

---

## 6. Delivery Performance Notes

- Apex Supply Co.'s current contract does not include a formal late-delivery penalty or delay-compensation clause. Delivery performance is instead tracked internally via the vendor reliability score (Section 7) and factored into future sourcing decisions and multi-quote evaluations.
- Repeated late deliveries (three or more instances within a rolling 90-day period) trigger a mandatory contract review per internal procurement policy.

---

## 7. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) Enforcement

- The flat 50-unit MOQ applies uniformly across all five contracted SKUs, regardless of category or unit price.
- Multiple SKUs may be combined on a single PO; each SKU line item must independently meet the 50-unit MOQ.

---

## 8. Vendor Reliability Notes

Apex Supply Co. holds a current internal reliability score of **7.5 / 10**, reflecting consistent mid-tier performance with occasional minor delays that have not historically triggered escalation thresholds. Apex is designated the **default vendor for standard, non-urgent hardware replenishment** (SKU-2001–SKU-2005) where neither TechCorp's premium speed nor GlobalParts' bulk pricing is required, per AutoStock-AI internal procurement policy.
