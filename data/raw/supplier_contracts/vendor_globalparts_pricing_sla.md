## 1. Vendor Profile

GlobalParts Direct is AutoStock-AI's primary bulk-volume supplier, offering the lowest per-unit pricing across the full ten-SKU catalog (both Electronics and Hardware categories) in exchange for significantly longer lead times and much higher minimum order quantities than TechCorp Logistics (VEND-001) or Apex Supply Co. (VEND-003). GlobalParts is best suited for **planned, non-urgent replenishment** where sufficient safety stock buffer exists to absorb extended lead times.

- **Standard Lead Time:** 14–21 business days from PO confirmation (varies by SKU and order volume tier — see Section 2)
- **Expedited Fulfillment:** Not offered under this contract tier
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** Varies by SKU, range 100–250 units (see per-SKU schedule below)
- **Payment Terms:** 2/10 Net 60 (2% discount if paid within 10 days of invoice; otherwise full amount due within 60 days)
- **Delay Compensation:** Fixed $200 credit per delayed PO (flat, not percentage-based — see Section 6)

## 4. Lead Time Structure

- Lead times scale **upward** with order volume tier, since higher-tier quantities are produced in dedicated manufacturing batches rather than pulled from standing warehouse stock.
- All lead times quoted are in business days from written PO confirmation and deposit receipt (see Section 5).
- No expedited fulfillment option exists under this contract. Urgent replenishment needs should be routed to VEND-001 (TechCorp Logistics) or split-sourced per internal procurement policy.

---

## 5. Payment Terms

- **Standard Terms:** 2/10 Net 60 — a 2% discount applies if the invoice is paid in full within 10 calendar days of the invoice date; otherwise the full invoice amount is due within 60 calendar days.
- **Deposit Requirement:** A 30% deposit is required at PO confirmation for all Tier 2 and Tier 3 volume orders before production/lead time begins.
- **Accepted Payment Methods:** Wire transfer only (international vendor; no card or ACH support).

---

## 6. Delay Compensation Clause

- **Compensation Structure:** Fixed **$200 credit per delayed purchase order**, regardless of order size or dollar value, applied as a credit toward the next invoice.
- **Trigger Condition:** Compensation is triggered once actual delivery exceeds the confirmed lead time (per Section 2 tier) by more than **5 business days**.
- **Cap:** One $200 credit per PO, non-cumulative regardless of total delay duration.
- **Comparison Note:** Unlike TechCorp Logistics' percentage-based penalty (1%/day, up to 10% of order value), GlobalParts' flat $200 compensation is proportionally minor for large-volume Tier 3 orders and should not be relied upon as a meaningful risk offset for high-value POs.

---

## 7. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) Enforcement

- MOQ thresholds in Section 2 are strictly enforced; GlobalParts does not accept sub-MOQ orders under any circumstances, including rush requests.
- Partial shipments against a single confirmed PO are permitted only for Tier 3 orders (500+/1000+ units), and must be pre-arranged in writing at PO confirmation.

GlobalParts is designated the **preferred vendor for planned bulk replenishment** where safety stock buffer exceeds the 14–21 business day lead time window, per AutoStock-AI internal procurement policy.
