## 1. Vendor Profile

TechCorp Logistics is AutoStock-AI's premium electronics supplier, positioned for **speed over cost**. TechCorp maintains dedicated safety stock for all five contracted SKUs and guarantees the fastest standard lead time of any vendor in the approved vendor list. TechCorp pricing carries a premium of approximately 15–30% over bulk-tier competitors (see VEND-002) in exchange for shorter lead times, lower minimum order quantities, and expedited fulfillment options.

- **Standard Lead Time:** 3–5 business days from PO confirmation
- **Expedited Lead Time:** 24 hours from PO confirmation (surcharge applies — see Section 4)
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** Varies by SKU, range 20–50 units (see per-SKU schedule below)
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 from invoice date
- **Late Delivery Penalty:** 1% of total order value per business day of delay, capped at 10% of order value

## 4. Expedited Fulfillment Terms

- **Expedited Lead Time:** 24 hours from confirmed PO, subject to per-SKU expedited quantity caps listed in Section 2.
- **Expedited Fee:** A flat **+25% surcharge** on the applicable tiered unit price for the full expedited quantity.
- **Expedited Eligibility:** Expedited terms apply only to in-stock inventory at TechCorp's regional fulfillment centers. Any portion of an order exceeding the per-SKU expedited cap reverts to standard 3–5 business day lead time at standard tiered pricing.
- **Expedited Order Cutoff:** Expedited requests must be confirmed before 2:00 PM local vendor time to qualify for same-cycle 24-hour dispatch.

## 5. Payment Terms

- **Standard Terms:** Net 30 from invoice date, invoice issued at time of shipment confirmation.
- **Accepted Payment Methods:** ACH, wire transfer, corporate purchasing card (2.5% processing surcharge applies to card payments).
- **Early Payment Discount:** None currently offered under this contract tier.

---

## 6. Late Delivery Penalty Clause

- **Penalty Rate:** 1% of total order value per business day of delay beyond the confirmed lead time window (standard or expedited, as applicable).
- **Penalty Cap:** Maximum penalty of 10% of total order value (i.e., penalty accrual stops at day 10 of delay).
- **Grace Period:** None — penalty accrual begins on the first business day following the missed delivery date.
- **Application:** Penalty is deducted from the corresponding invoice as a line-item credit; it is not issued as a separate refund.
- **Force Majeure Exception:** Penalty clause is suspended for delays attributable to documented force majeure events (natural disaster, customs seizure, carrier network failure) with written notice provided within 48 hours of the delay event.

---

## 7. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) Enforcement

- Orders below the per-SKU MOQ listed in Section 2 will not be accepted as standalone purchase orders.
- Multiple SKUs may be combined on a single PO to meet overall order minimums, but each SKU's individual MOQ still applies to that SKU's line item.
- MOQ does not apply to expedited replenishment orders placed under an active backorder agreement already covering the base MOQ.
